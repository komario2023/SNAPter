SNAPter - all keys in one place
================================

Where a key can be changed is written after it. Keys marked "fixed" are built into the program (the standard ones: copy, save, undo...).
A key name in the settings can be a letter, a digit, F1-F12 or the name of any key (Tab, Space, Oem3...). Oem3 is the key left of 1:
Yo on a Russian keyboard, ` and ~ on an English one.


1. Global keys (work everywhere in Windows)
-------------------------------------------
Settings, page "Hotkeys". Every action has its own field and an on/off switch.
  Ctrl+Shift+Alt+R             region (the main one)
  Ctrl+Shift+Alt+F             the whole screen to a file
  Ctrl+Shift+Alt+C             the whole screen to the clipboard
  Ctrl+Shift+Alt+G             record a GIF
  Ctrl+Shift+Alt+M             record a video
  Ctrl+Shift+Alt+S             scrolling screenshot
(these are the defaults; each can be changed or switched off)


2. In the editor, while a region is being chosen or edited
-----------------------------------------------------------
Can be changed:
  Tool keys (V A L R O B M T N U E I F S Z)       Settings, page "Editor keys"
  Preset keys (1 ... 9)                            Settings, page "Editor keys"
  Switch the profile (Oem3 = Yo / ` by default)    Settings, page "Profiles" (key field and the order of the profiles)
  Aspect ratio of the region (Tab; with Shift - back)  Settings, page "Aspect ratios" (key field)
  Move the panels to the next corner inside a big region (Shift+Tab; or CapsLock, Tab, F1, none)
                                                   Settings, page "Panels for a big region"
  What Shift, Ctrl, Alt (also the right Ctrl / Shift / Alt) do while drawing and on a tap
                                                   Settings, page "Modifier keys"
  What Enter does (alone and with Shift / Ctrl / Alt)  Settings, page "Sizes and snapping"
  The key that makes a dragged object stick to the axes of others  Settings, page "Sizes and snapping"
  The key of the tray icon click with Shift         Settings, page "Sizes and snapping" (tray block)

Fixed (standard keys):
  Esc            step back: the selected object, then the tool, then the whole capture
  Enter          the action of the Enter key (see above)
  Ctrl+C         copy the picture          Ctrl+S         save          Ctrl+Shift+S   save as
  Ctrl+P         pin on the screen         Ctrl+U         upload        Ctrl+B         beautiful screenshot on/off
  Ctrl+A         the whole screen becomes the region  Ctrl+D         duplicate     Delete / Backspace  delete the selected
  Ctrl+Z         undo                      Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z      redo
  [ and ]        the selected object one layer down / up
  Arrows         move the selected object (or the region) by 1 px, with Shift by 10 px
  Space          while the region is being drawn: move the whole frame
  Mouse wheel    thickness / size (with Shift, Ctrl or Alt held - the strength of that key's action)


3. The profile key in detail
-----------------------------
The key works before the region is drawn, while it is drawn and after, for this capture only (nothing is saved). The profiles go in the order of the
list on the settings page "Profiles" (Full, Ascetic, Minimal and your own).
- While you have not changed the order, a capture starts with your saved setup (the profile marked with a dot on that page; when none matches, with the
  setup as it is saved) and the key goes on down the list: Full, Ascetic, Minimal... and round.
- Once you change the order on the page (arrows or dragging the name) every capture starts with the FIRST profile of the list, and the key goes down
  the list from it. Put your own profile at the top to make it the one every capture starts with.
Inside a text that is being typed, in a field with a number, or in a color field the key is a normal key (it types the character).
